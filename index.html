Offline deliveries

Type the pick up and drop off addresses. The tool finds the coordinates and calculates the driving distance in km. Then copy the rows and paste them into Excel.

Coordinates come from OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) and the road distance from the public OSRM server. If a road route is not available, the km shows the straight-line distance and says so. Check the first few results on a map before relying on them.
